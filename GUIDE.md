## Step 4: Create Your First Short
Prompt Antigravity with your video idea:
```text
Make a viral 35-second Tall Tale 3D video about:
The man who bought an entire bank for $1.
Hook the viewer in the first 2 seconds!
```

### What Happens Automatically:
1. ✍️ **Script**: Generates a high-retention script with a strong hook.
2. 🎙️ **Voiceover**: Generates an energetic voiceover.
3. ⏱️ **Subtitles**: Syncs every word with captions.
4. 🎨 **Visuals**: Animates characters, camera moves, and scene transitions.
5. 🔊 **Audio**: Mixes sound effects and background music.
6. 🎬 **Export**: Delivers a ready-to-post `.mp4` file.

## 🚀 Pro Retention Secrets (1M+ Views)
1. **Cut the Intro**: Never start with *"Today we are looking at..."*. Start immediately with the punchline hook.
2. **Pacing**: Change visual angles or character expressions every 2 seconds.
3. **Seamless Looping**: Instruct the engine to make the final sentence naturally loop back into the opening hook.
4. **Custom Voice**: Drop your own phone voice memo into the folder and tell Antigravity to sync the animation to your audio.
