# Claude & Human Viral 3D Shorts Generator

This skill teaches the agent how to write, scaffold, and generate viral 9:16 vertical shorts (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) featuring **3D character models of Claude AI and a Human character** having rapid-fire dialogue about fascinating true stories, legal loopholes, science glitches, and crazy mysteries.

Works **100% locally with zero API costs, zero monthly subscriptions, and zero external dependencies**.

---

## 🍎 How to Install on Your MacBook

You don't need to copy any project repo. Simply copy this single `SKILL.md` file to your Mac!

### Option A: Available for ALL projects on your Mac (Global Skill)
Open Terminal on your Mac and run:
```bash
# For Antigravity:
mkdir -p ~/.gemini/config/skills/claude-human-shorts
cp SKILL.md ~/.gemini/config/skills/claude-human-shorts/

# Or for Claude Code:
mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills/claude-human-shorts
cp SKILL.md ~/.claude/skills/claude-human-shorts/
```

### Option B: For any single project folder on your Mac
In any project folder on your Mac:
```bash
mkdir -p .agents/skills/claude-human-shorts
cp SKILL.md .agents/skills/claude-human-shorts/
```

---

## 🎬 How to Trigger on Your Mac

Once the skill is in place, you simply open Antigravity (or Claude Code) on your Mac and type:

> *"Generate a viral 3D Claude & Human short about the man who bought a bank for $1 and open the studio."*

The AI will automatically:
1. Write the high-retention dialogue script formatted with `[Human]` and `[Claude]`.
2. Scaffold the standalone `index.html` with full 3D models into your Mac folder.
3. Run `open index.html` to launch it directly in your browser!

## ✍️ Viral Retention Script Blueprint

* **Duration**: 30–45 seconds.
* **0–2s Punchy Hook**: No introduction, jump straight into the shocking claim.
* **Banter**: Inquisitive Human avatar vs. calm, witty Claude AI delivering verified facts.
* **Seamless Loop**: The final punchline connects grammatically back into the opening hook.

#### Standard Script Format:
```text
[Human]
Claude, is it actually true that a man bought a billion-dollar bank for just ONE dollar?

[Claude]
Not only is it true, but the United States government practically begged him to take it.

[Human]
Wait, what?! How is that even legally possible?

[Claude]
During the 2008 crash, Texas billionaire Andy Beal saw a collapsing bank drowning in toxic debt. He walked in, slid a single one-dollar bill across the desk, and signed for it.

[Human]
Did he actually make any profit off that?

[Claude]
Three years later, he liquidated the distressed assets and walked away with over two point five billion dollars in pure profit!

[Human]
That might be the greatest legal heist in human history!
```

## 🎨 3D Character Model Specifications

* **Human Model**:
  * 3D gradient-shaded hoodie (`#1d273b` to `#2b3954`) with realistic collar and drawstrings.
  * Expressive animated face with blush cheeks and natural head tilting.
  * Natural eye blinking cycle (~every 3 seconds) with specular gloss reflections.
  * Phoneme mouth cavity opening to speech volume with teeth and tongue.
  * Studio condenser microphone capsule in foreground.

* **Claude AI Model**:
  * Matte-black porcelain chassis with brushed bronze bevels and glowing neon orange conduits (`#d97706`).
  * 8-point geometric Anthropic spark reactor core in the chest.
  * Curved digital visor with glowing optical sensors and dynamic laser matrix scan line.
  * Neon frequency-modulated audio equalizer mouth dancing to speech.
  * Anti-gravity hover animation.
